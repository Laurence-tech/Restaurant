Our story

Cooked over
one element.

Ember & Oak opened with a single restriction: everything on the plate touches fire. No shortcuts, no gas rings backstage — just wood, coals, and a kitchen that plans each menu around what's in season.

9Years open
4Seasonal menus / yr
40k+Covers served

Head chef

Elena Marchetti

"Fire doesn't forgive a bad ingredient. It just tells you the truth about it — that's why we cook this way."

Elena trained across Italy and the Basque coast before opening Ember & Oak's kitchen in 2016. She sources within a 150km radius wherever possible and rebuilds the menu with every change of season.

  • Eat Cape Town — Chef of the Year, 2023
  • 15 years cooking over open flame
